Research

Systems research for the AI era

Our work builds on a long-standing foundation in multimedia systems, real-time/embedded systems, storage, and power-aware computing, with current emphasis on AI-driven system optimization.
01

AI Systems & RAG

Efficient on-premises AI inference, retrieval-augmented generation, adaptive retrieval, reranking, and system-level optimization for generative models.

02

Machine-Centric Video & Edge AI

QoA-aware streaming, video analytics, edge/cloud collaboration, and multi-node orchestration designed for machine perception rather than only human viewing.

03

Intelligent Storage & Caching

Video and vector storage, hot/cold data placement, adaptive replication, edge caching, SSD-aware allocation, and data placement under capacity constraints.

04

Resource & Energy Optimization

DRL and optimization techniques for compute, storage, bandwidth, power, and latency management across heterogeneous edge and cloud systems.

Current Projects

What we are working on now

Two current projects connect System Software Lab.'s systems expertise with AI inference, machine-centric video, and on-premises generative AI.
National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)
Mar 2026 – Feb 2029

Development of QoA (Quality-of-Analysis)-Aware Machine-Centric Video Streaming and Multi-Node Orchestration Technologies for AI Inference Systems

This project develops machine-centric video streaming and multi-node orchestration techniques that jointly optimize representation quality, network transfer, and compute placement for downstream AI inference. QoA-aware control adapts video quality and resource allocation to preserve analysis accuracy while reducing end-to-end latency, bandwidth, and computation overhead.

Machine-Centric VideoQuality-of-AnalysisAI InferenceMulti-Node OrchestrationEdge ComputingResource Optimization
Institute for Information & Communications Technology Planning & Evaluation (IITP)
Apr 2026 – Dec 2029

Human–AI Co-Evolution Talent Development for Advancing On-Premises Generative Model Technologies in the Financial Sector

This project advances secure and efficient on-premises generative AI for financial environments while training researchers who can bridge AI models and system software. System Software Lab. focuses on system-level topics including efficient inference, RAG pipelines, resource management, storage and retrieval optimization, and practical deployment of generative models in constrained on-premises environments.
